About the Project

Health360 uses data visualization to explore patient demographics, health indicators, and patterns in the healthcare dataset.

Our Focus

Understand patient information and present healthcare data in a clear and accessible way. This project is intended for educational analysis and is not a medical diagnostic tool.

What We Explore

  • Patient age groups and demographics
  • Diabetes, heart disease, and stroke indicators
  • BMI categories and exercise levels
  • Blood pressure and other health metrics
